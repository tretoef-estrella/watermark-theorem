# Licence

This repository holds two kinds of work, and each has the licence that suits it.

| What | Licence |
|---|---|
| **Code**: the Lean project and every script (`.lean`, `.py`, `.m2`, `.sh`) | **MIT** — the text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE) |
| **Texts**: the papers, the certificates, the pencil proof, the story, the logs and every other document | **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** — [legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode) |

## What CC BY 4.0 allows, and what it asks

You may copy, redistribute, translate, adapt and build upon these texts, for any purpose, including commercially. In return you must:

1. **give appropriate credit** — name the author, **Rafael Amichis Luengo** (cited as *Amichis Luengo, R.*), and cite the work as in [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff);
2. **link to the licence**;
3. **say whether you changed anything**, and not suggest that the author endorses your use.

## What MIT allows, and what it asks

You may use, copy, modify, merge, publish and distribute the code freely, provided the copyright notice and the permission notice in [LICENSE](LICENSE) are kept in every copy or substantial portion of it.

## Scholarly use

A licence governs copying; scholarly practice governs credit. If a result, an idea or a number from this repository is used in research — whether or not any text or code is copied — please cite it.

## What is not covered

- The papers of other authors are **not** in this repository; they are only cited.
- The texts are provided **as is**, without warranty that they are free of errors. The status of each document is the one it states.
